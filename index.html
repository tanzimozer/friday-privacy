Friday — Privacy Policy

A private, single-user assistant. Last updated 9 October 2026.

What this is

Friday is a personal assistant built and run by Tanzim Ozer for his own use. It is not a product, it is not offered to anyone else, and it has no other users. This policy exists because the WHOOP Developer Platform requires every application to publish one.

Whose data it handles

Only Tanzim Ozer's own data, accessed with his own account and his own consent. No other person's WHOOP data is requested, received or stored.

What is accessed

  • Profile and body measurements
  • Sleep, recovery and daily cycle summaries
  • Workout and strain records

These are used for one purpose: to let the assistant give its owner training and recovery guidance.

Where it is stored

On hardware owned by Tanzim Ozer. Nothing is sold, shared, published, or sent to any advertiser, data broker or analytics service. Access tokens are stored locally and are not transmitted to any third party other than WHOOP itself when making an authorised request.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while it is useful and can be deleted at any time by its owner. Access can be revoked at any time from the WHOOP account settings, which immediately ends this application's access.
